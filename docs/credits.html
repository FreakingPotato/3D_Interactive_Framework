Scientific examples

Covert Lab / vEcoli (MIT); underlying model: Macklin et al., Science (2020). This demo uses our precomputed model output; its 3D molecular positions are illustrative.

Thornburg, Maytin et al., Bringing the genetically minimal cell to life on a computer in 4D, Cell (2026). Correction. Thornburg & Maytin, Zenodo v1 dataset (2025): CC BY 4.0. We extracted 0–60 seconds, retained all spatial particles and voxel coordinates, and converted them to compressed JSON with derived surfaces. Shapes and orientations remain illustrative.

Predicted protein structures: Google DeepMind / EMBL-EBI AlphaFold DB, CC BY 4.0. Recolored and converted to surface meshes; individual records are in the included structure catalog.
